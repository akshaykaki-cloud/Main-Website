Questions

Straight answers.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Monthly plans are month-to-month with 30 days’ notice. One-time setups are paid when we start, and you approve the work before it goes live.

How does the AI call assistant work?

Calls forward to the assistant when you’re busy or closed — or always, if you prefer. It answers naturally, handles common questions, books into your calendar and texts you a summary. Anything unusual comes straight to you.

Can I start small?

That’s how most clients start — a website or chatbot first, then booking or marketing when it makes sense. Add a second service and setup is 15% off.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. Tell us how your business works in plain language. We set it up and show you how to use it.

How long does it take?

Assistants and booking: usually a few days. Websites: one to two weeks. Custom builds get a timeline with their quote.

Is my data safe?

Your information is only used to run your tools, and stays private to your business.
